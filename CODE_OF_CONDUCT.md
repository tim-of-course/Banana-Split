# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Welcome people regardless of
experience or background, discuss ideas rather than people, and give feedback
with the goal of improving the project.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

These standards apply in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and
other spaces where someone is representing the Banana Split project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through their
GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed in confidence. Maintainers may edit or
remove contributions or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
behavior is inconsistent with these standards.
